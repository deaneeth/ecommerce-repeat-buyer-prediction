E-Commerce Repeat Buyer Prediction — Production Analytics Presentation Guide 
2025/2026 Academic Year 
Presentation (P1) Recording Evaluation Guidelines 
Total Time Duration – 40 mins (per group)  
Platform – Plymouth Zoom Meeting  
Instructions  
• Set up a Zoom meeting with your group members where all members should 
participate and record the entire session.  
• During the presentation/demonstration, you should keep your video switched on.  
• At the beginning of your presentation, you should introduce yourself with your 
Name and Plymouth index/ID number.  
Your presentation recording will be evaluated based on the following:-  
1. Introduction (4 mins – Group) – 5 Marks 
Students must clearly explain: 
• Selected industry & dataset  
• Business problems they are solving  
• Why this problem is important  
Example: 
“Customer churn prediction helps telecom companies reduce revenue loss.” 
2. Data & NoSQL Explanation (3 mins) – 5 Marks 
Students must: 
• Explain dataset structure  
• Show MongoDB sample documents  
• Explain:  
o Why NoSQL?  
o Schema flexibility  
o Scalability  
Must include screenshots or live demo 
3. Spark Data Processing (10 mins) – 10 Marks 
Students must demonstrate: 
• Loading dataset in Databricks  
• Key transformations:  
Ex: 
o Cleaning  
o Filtering  
o GroupBy / Aggregations  
MOST IMPORTANT: 
• Explain what each analysis means  
Not just code 
Focus on insights 
4. Data Visualization (3 mins) – 5 Marks 
Students must present: 
• At least 3 charts  
• Explain:  
o What does this show?  
o Why is it important?  
Example: 
“This chart shows peak sales hours → helps staffing decisions” 
5. Machine Learning (7 mins) – 7 Marks 
Students must explain: 
• Selected algorithm (why this one?)  
• Features used  
• Model results  
Must explain: 
• Accuracy / Precision / Recall (or RMSE etc.)  
• What does the model prediction mean in real life? 
6. Big Data Architecture (3 mins) – 3 Marks 
Students must explain: 
• Full pipeline:  
o Data source  
o Storage  
o Processing  
o ML  
o Output  
“How data flows in real industry systems” 
7. Final Insights & Business Impact (5 mins) – 10 Marks 
Students must: 
• Summarize key findings  
• Explain business decisions  
• Explain real-world impact

8. Individual Contribution (5 mins total OR ~1 min each) – 5 Marks 
Each student must: 
• Explain their contribution  
• Show what they implemented  
• Mention:  
o Challenges faced  
o How they solved them  
Important Rules to Add 
• Explain insights like a data analyst  
• Connect everything to business value  
• Insights must be supported by analysis results (charts, statistics, or model outputs) 
• Each student must answer at least one question  
• Marks may be adjusted based on understanding 
“Your goal is NOT to show code. 
Your goal is to convince a company to use your solution.”
